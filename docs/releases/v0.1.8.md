# M45 Drive Watcher 0.1.8

Drive labels now lead the four-column overview. The selected drive has a formatted Summary with status, temperature, monitoring, capacity and connection. Technical details contain device paths, serials, endurance/spare readings, volume and counter tables, and expandable diagnostics. The tray also uses drive labels first.

Unsupported drives and USB drives with inaccessible SMART default to monitoring off. Explicit saved choices are preserved. The status wording remains “SMART unavailable.” No USB driver settings or SMART collection commands change.
